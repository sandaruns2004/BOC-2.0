ACME CORP - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & KNOWLEDGE BASE
Last Updated: October 2026

1. TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY
1.1 Flights: All employees must book economy class tickets for domestic travel. For international travel exceeding 8 hours, business class may be booked with prior approval from a VP.
1.2 Hotels: The maximum nightly hotel rate is $250 in major cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo) and $150 elsewhere.
1.3 Meals: Employees are allotted a daily per diem of $75 for meals while traveling. Receipts are only required for individual meals exceeding $25.
1.4 Reimbursement: All expenses must be submitted via the Concur platform within 30 days of the trip's conclusion.

2. IT SECURITY & DEVICE POLICY
2.1 Passwords: Must be at least 14 characters long, containing upper, lower, numbers, and symbols. Passwords expire every 90 days.
2.2 Hardware: Employees are issued a standard MacBook Pro or Dell XPS. Upgrades require manager approval and a valid business case (e.g., software engineering, video editing).
2.3 Data Classification:
- Public: Safe for external release (e.g., marketing materials).
- Internal: Safe for all employees, but not external (e.g., company all-hands slides).
- Confidential: Restricted to specific teams (e.g., unreleased financial data, HR records).
2.4 USB Drives: The use of unencrypted personal USB drives on company laptops is strictly prohibited.

3. HUMAN RESOURCES & TIME OFF
3.1 PTO: Full-time employees accrue 20 days of Paid Time Off annually. Up to 5 days may roll over to the following calendar year.
3.2 Sick Leave: Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
3.3 Parental Leave: Acme Corp offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for primary and secondary caregivers following the birth or adoption of a child.
3.4 Core Hours: While we operate on a flexible schedule, all employees are expected to be online and available for meetings during the core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local timezone.

4. PRODUCT & ENGINEERING
4.1 Deployment: No production deployments are allowed on Fridays without explicitly documented VP approval (The "No-Deploy Friday" rule).
4.2 Code Reviews: All pull requests must be approved by at least two senior engineers before merging to the main branch.
4.3 SLAs: The engineering team maintains a 99.99% uptime SLA for external APIs. Priority 1 (P1) incidents require an initial response within 15 minutes.

5. ESCALATION MATRIX
5.1 Tier 1: General queries and basic troubleshooting (handled by AgentForge AI).
5.2 Tier 2: Account lockouts and billing disputes (Human Operations Team).
5.3 Tier 3: Security breaches and infrastructure outages (On-call Engineering Team).
