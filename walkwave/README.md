# Walkwave demo materials

Walkwave is a fictional Sri Lankan shoe company used to demonstrate AgentForge's floating chat widget. All business details, products, prices, and orders here are sample data for the hackathon.

## Files

- `company.md`: company identity and presentation scope.
- `website-content.md`: website sections, product catalogue, and chatbot copy.
- `policies.md`: company knowledge document for PDF/TXT ingestion.
- `demo-database.md`: proposed Firestore records, customer mapping, and demo questions.

## Demo story

Upload the policy document, open the Walkwave website, click the chat bubble, ask about returns, retrieve the signed-in customer's shoe order, request an email update, and escalate a large refund to the existing admin screen.

These files prepare content only. They do not create the website, upload knowledge, seed Firestore, or change application behavior. Prices and policies are proposed demo defaults and can be adjusted before implementation.

Walkwave represents the ready-made widget integration. The second company's enterprise API demonstration remains separate; its name and materials are still to be chosen.
